1. Project Overview & Goal

The GZS Tool calculates a Generative Zenith Score and offers an actionable, paid Zenith Action Plan. Target users: Small Business Owners and SEO Professionals.

3. The Generative Zenith Score (GZS) Calculation

Aggregate score out of 100 built from weighted sub-scores.

Authority & Content Depth

35%

E-E-A-T signals, content completeness, and structure for AI synthesis.

Intent & Answer Efficiency

35%

Measures how quickly and directly the page answers high-value user queries.

Trust, Speed, & Technical Health

30%

Core Web Vitals, security posture, and foundational technical SEO.

Immediate insights displayed in three distinct zones.

Zone 1: Overview

Focus: High-Level Status

  • Sub-score grades (A–F)
  • Personalized Generative Summary paragraph

Source: Report Card (Idea 2)

Zone 2: Immediate Fixes

Focus: Quick Wins / Urgency

  • Top 3 Critical Action Items
  • Examples: Missing Local Schema, Slowest Page $X$

Source: Fix-It-Fast (Idea 1)

Zone 3: Strategic Planning

Focus: Prioritized roadmap

Simplified table with 3–5 recommendations categorized by Impact (High/Medium) and Difficulty (Low/Medium).

Source: Opportunity Matrix (Idea 3)

5. Premium Deliverable: Zenith Action Plan

Unlocked after payment and packed with proprietary checks and competitive intelligence.

GZS Sub-Score Proprietary Check Required Data Output
Authority Semantic Velocity Score Current score, benchmarks, and URLs/claims needing validation.
Authority Generative Citation Structure Check Specific facts/data points needing micro-formatting.

5.2 Premium Competitive & Technical Data
  • Generative Share of Voice (G-SOV) Comparison: Chart comparing G-SOV vs. 3 anonymous competitors for top topics.
  • Prompt Alignment Efficiency (PAE) Gap Analysis: Table of top 5 missed conversational queries, competitor PAE, and current PAE.
  • Generative Bot Traffic & Crawl Blockers: Prioritized technical errors with line-of-code specificity (e.g., file + line number).
  • Multimodal Content Performance Index (MCPI) Check: Transcript status and VideoObject schema for top 5 media assets.
